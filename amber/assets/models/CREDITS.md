# Model credits

The `.glb` files in this folder are photo-scanned and hand-made props from
[Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), released under **CC0** (public domain, no
attribution required, credited here anyway). Textures were downscaled (1024 px
for large props, 512 px for small ones) and packed into single `.glb` files.
